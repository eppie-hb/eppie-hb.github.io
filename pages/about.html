About

I'm a product manager working in digital media, streaming and OTT. I spent ten years inside broadcast operations before moving into product six years ago, and that combination is the thing I lead with: I know how these systems actually behave in production, so the roadmaps I write survive contact with the people who have to run them.

How I work

Most of my work sits where content operations, engineering and commercial priorities collide, migrating platforms without dropping service, launching on-demand products across regions, retiring tooling that quietly costs a fortune. I care about making the trade-offs explicit rather than optimistic, and I would rather ship a narrower thing that works than a broad thing that needs an asterisk.

Practically, that looks like: getting close to the operational detail before committing to a plan, keeping a small number of measurable goals rather than a long feature list, and doing the unglamorous stakeholder work early so delivery isn't a negotiation.

Where I've done it

Currently product manager for Global News at Corus Entertainment, where I've led the rebrand across digital and OTT platforms, grown the video site, and launched daily news VOD across eight regions. Before that, product roles at Rogers Communications covering broadband tiers, IPTV and video on demand, including 15+ on-demand service launches and Canada's fastest fibre-to-the-home tier. Earlier still, broadcast production and post-production at Corus and Foxtel in Sydney.

Outside work

Long walks, a steady reading habit, and more online courses than I strictly need. I'm also the person on the team most likely to check in on you, which I've come to think of as part of the job rather than separate from it.
